Find any Bluetooth device nearby by its signal strength: warmer, colder, found it.
Lost your earbuds, the tracker on your keys or the TV remote? Find My Anything turns your Pebble into a Bluetooth finder. The companion Android app scans for devices nearby, the watch lists them and guides you to the one you pick. Your phone can stay in your pocket.

NEEDS THE FREE ANDROID APP (not in the Play Store): get it at github.com/n0va-SIDEffects/pebble-findmyanything/releases, the settings page links to it. Android only, no iPhone.

Features
- Devices nearby, strongest first: trackers (AirTag, SmartTag, Tile), earbuds, phones, computers, watches, TVs, sensors
- Search screen: big signal number and a band that fills up as you get closer, green to red on colour screens; an arrow shows whether you are getting closer
- Buzzes faster the closer you get; Time 2 and Pebble 2 Duo beep as well
- Filter by type, a calm list that holds still while you scroll, touch on Time 2 and Round 2
- Settings in the Pebble app or in the phone app
- 7 languages: English, Deutsch, Français, Italiano, Español, Português, Nederlands

Buttons
List: Select searches the device, hold Select to filter.
Search: Select switches sound/vibration, Up resets the best value, Down turns on the backlight.

Good to know: signal strength tells you warmer or colder, not an exact distance. Only devices that are switched on and sending can be found; connected earbuds often stay silent.
